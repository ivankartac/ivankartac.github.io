About

Hi! 👋 I am a researcher in natural language processing (NLP), currently working as a Ph.D. candidate at the Institute of Formal and Applied Linguistics at Charles University, Prague. I am interested in evaluation, explainability, and reasoning, specifically in how to reliably evaluate (language) models and better understand their behavior and capabilities in compositional and interactive settings. I am advised by Ondřej Dušek and Mateusz Lango. Previously, I worked as a software engineer at various ML/NLP startups. I got my B.S. in General Linguistics and Phonetics and M.S. in Computer Science at Charles University.
